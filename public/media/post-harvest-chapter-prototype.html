02
ReframeFrom brief to actual need
[ 04 / FINDING THE FOCUS ]

Finding the focus

We were assigned grain storage. The research pointed somewhere else.

The gap was knowledge, not hardware.

Farmers named drying and storage as visible problems. The interviews revealed a latent need: knowing how and when the existing system should be used.

03
DevelopConcepts tested with farmers
[ 06 / CONCEPT DEVELOPMENT ]

Developing with farmers

We took the concepts back to the farmers twice.

ROUND ONE

Ideas shown to three farmers, each of us sketching in our own hand. They preferred a drying table.

ROUND TWO

Three concepts, all redrawn by one person, so the farmers judged ideas and not drawings.

The Drying Tower received the strongest response, so we developed it further.
